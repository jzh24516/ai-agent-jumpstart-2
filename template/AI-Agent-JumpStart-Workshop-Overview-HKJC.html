AI Agent JumpStart Workshop

A hands-on, interactive workshop to explore, design, and build AI agents with GitHub Copilot and Microsoft Copilot Studio.

No cost — first session Half day · 4–5 hours 20–30 participants 6 guided labs In-person preferred 3+ Microsoft coaches

The AI Agent JumpStart Workshop is a hands-on, interactive experience — not a classroom training — that guides your team through the newest GitHub Copilot and Copilot Studio agent (Harness) capabilities across six guided labs (the lab contents with multilingual support). Participants finish the session having identified their own prioritized “Hero Use Cases” — the highest-value agent scenarios to take forward.

Program at a glance

Cost & investment

  • The first workshop, hosted by Microsoft, is delivered at no cost — an interactive workshop, not a paid training.
  • Microsoft account and specialist teams take part as coaches and help surface follow-up opportunities and next steps.
  • If you later choose to host the workshop yourselves, Copilot credits and Power Platform / Copilot Studio licensing would apply.

Class size & setup

  • Ideal group of up to 20 participants; 30 maximum for the best experience.
  • A classroom-style room with power available at every seat.
  • Each participant brings a personal or corporate laptop.
  • Access to the workshop environment is via Microsoft-provided user credentials.

Logistics & format

  • In-person is recommended; Microsoft facilitates on site (travel permitting) or remotely.
  • Half-day session — approximately 4 to 5 hours.
  • At least three Microsoft coaches on site to guide participants.
  • Before the session, share the attendee email list plus your branding — logo, company / business-unit name, and contact person(s) — for a customized welcome page.

Commitments & eligibility

  • No hard pipeline threshold or commitment is required to qualify.
  • Scheduling is subject to the availability of a suitable date.
  • After the first session, the workshop can move to a customer-run, in-house cadence — ideal for an AI acceleration or innovation team.

What's included

Microsoft provides

  • Expert facilitation and at least three on-site coaches.
  • The hands-on lab environment and participant credentials.
  • A customized, co-branded workshop welcome page.
  • Guidance to capture and prioritize your Hero Use Cases.

You provide

  • A classroom-style venue with power at every seat.
  • Participant laptops (personal or corporate) able to reach the lab environment.
  • The attendee email list, shared ahead of the session.
  • Branding assets: logo, company / BU name, and contact person(s).

Ready to run a session?

Talk to your Microsoft account team to confirm a date, venue, and participant list — and to plan the path to an in-house cadence after your first workshop.
